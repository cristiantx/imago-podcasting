## Design Context

### Users
Imago serves solo podcasters and small podcast teams who need a fast way to search their transcript archive without losing production momentum. They are usually trying to relocate a specific discussion, decision, quote, or moment inside a past episode and jump directly to the right timestamp.

### Brand Personality
Modern, playful, and friendly. The interface should feel capable without feeling cold, and polished without reading as enterprise-heavy. The emotional goal is confidence with momentum: users should feel like the app helps them move quickly, not manage a complicated system.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use bright, optimistic surfaces with soft contrast, rounded geometry, and clear motion cues that make search feel active and responsive. Favor a contemporary editorial workspace over a dark technical console. Avoid generic AI styling, heavy glassmorphism, and visually dense “ops dashboard” patterns.

### Design Principles
1. Make the next useful action obvious, especially when jumping to the right episode moment.
2. Lead with clarity and confirmation: show enough playback and transcript context to build confidence before navigation.
3. Keep the interface warm and lightweight even when the data is dense.
4. Use playful visual accents to reward interaction, not to compete with the content.
5. Preserve speed across solo and small-team workflows by reducing friction, ambiguity, and unnecessary modal steps.
